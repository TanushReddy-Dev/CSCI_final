Why this decision threshold?

Standard classification defaults to a naive 0.50 threshold. In civil infrastructure risk management, the asymmetric cost of an unflagged defect greatly exceeds the cost of a false alarm.

Screening Policy Protocol: The threshold deliberately prioritizes recall because this is a rapid surface screening tool. Results in the uncertain probability band 0.45 – 0.60 are automatically routed to human manual triage rather than treated as definitive classifications.
