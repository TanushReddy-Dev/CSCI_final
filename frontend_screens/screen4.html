Exclusions & Non-Certifications
MANDATORY ADVISORY

The following diagnostic responsibilities remain strictly within human professional engineering jurisdiction:

  • do_not_disturb_on
    No Structural Capacity or Stability Certification ConcreteGuard does NOT determine structural load capacity, seismic integrity, rebar load path, or immediate risk of collapse.
  • do_not_disturb_on
    No Subsurface or Volumetric Void Detection Does NOT penetrate beyond the optical surface layer. Cannot detect rebar oxidation, internal honeycombing, or shear delamination.
  • do_not_disturb_on
    No Replacement for Licensed PE Sign-Off Inferences cannot replace state-stamped engineering inspection certifications or OSHA/DOT regulatory reports.
  • do_not_disturb_on
    No Absolute Crack Depth or Aperture Metric Visual pixel dimensions are uncalibrated without co-registered laser profiling or tactile feeler gauges.
